Place the official Algoryx Community asset here as:

  algoryx-community-robot.glb

Source: https://www.algoryx.in/3d-assets/robot/

1. Create or sign in to an Algoryx account.
2. Open the Community 3D Assets section and download the robot asset (or another asset of your choice).
3. Rename/export the file to algoryx-community-robot.glb and place it in this folder (public/models/).
4. Delete this .txt file once the real asset is in place.

Until the real file is added, the site renders a clearly-labeled procedural placeholder model — it is NOT presented as the official Algoryx asset.
